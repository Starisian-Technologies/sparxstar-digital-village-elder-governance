## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose

1.1.1 This document establishes the process and procedural requirements for the intake of contributor works into the DVE governance pipeline. Intake is the process by which a work transitions from a contributor submission into a governed artifact subject to this governance framework.

1.1.2 Every intake operation must comply with the consent, classification, and provenance requirements established in this document and in AIWA-DOC-01 (Document Classification and Governance Artifact Taxonomy).

### 1.2 Scope

1.2.1 This document applies to all intake operations processed through an authorised DVE intake channel. An authorised intake channel is a channel designated by AIWA for the receipt of contributor submissions.

---

## 2. Intake Requirements

### 2.1 Pre-Intake Verification

2.1.1 Before a work may enter the DVE governance pipeline, the following must be verified:

(a) The intake channel is an AIWA-authorised intake channel;
(b) The submitter is an eligible contributor (an individual submitting their own work, or a community-authorised representative submitting on behalf of a community);
(c) Applicable consent has been obtained and recorded.

### 2.2 Consent Recording

2.2.1 Contributor consent must be recorded in the ArtifactGovernanceDeclaration at the time of intake. The consent record must specify:

(a) The identity of the consenting party (contributor or community-authorised representative);
(b) The specific uses consented to;
(c) Any temporal limits on consent;
(d) Any applicable Group Policy constraints;
(e) The date and channel through which consent was given.

### 2.3 Initial Classification

2.3.1 At intake, every work must be assigned an initial artifact category in accordance with AIWA-DOC-01 (Governance Artifact Taxonomy). The initial category determines the default dataset classification tier and cultural restriction status applicable to the artifact.

2.3.2 Community representatives submitting folklore or TCEs must provide the applicable Group Policy declaration at intake. Absent a Group Policy declaration, the work is assigned individual contributor defaults, which may not adequately protect community rights.

### 2.4 Provenance Documentation

2.4.1 The following provenance information must be recorded at intake:

(a) The identity of the contributor or community-authorised representative;
(b) The date and channel of submission;
(c) The declared origin of the work (geographic, community, linguistic context);
(d) Any known prior publication, distribution, or use of the work;
(e) Any encumbrances, third-party rights, or prior consent obligations known to the contributor at the time of submission.

---

## 3. Post-Intake Processing

### 3.1 Sieve Evaluation

3.1.1 All artifacts entering the DVE governance pipeline pass through the Sieve (Mḗh₁n̥s governance enforcement gate) as the first processing step after intake classification. The Sieve evaluates the artifact against applicable AIWA governance policies before any further processing occurs.

3.1.2 Artifacts that clear the Sieve receive a Release Receipt and proceed to the active DVE pipeline. Artifacts that fail the Sieve are placed in QUARANTINE pending governance resolution by AIWA.

### 3.2 ArtifactGovernanceDeclaration Finalisation

3.2.1 Upon completion of intake and initial Sieve evaluation, the ArtifactGovernanceDeclaration is finalised and sealed. The finalised ArtifactGovernanceDeclaration is the permanent, immutable governance record for the artifact.
