## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose

1.1.1 This document establishes Starisian Technologies' patent and intellectual property governance policy applicable to the SPARXSTAR platform, DVE operational stack, and the governance technology assets described within this repository.

1.1.2 This policy defines Starisian's authority over its proprietary technology assets and the boundaries of patent-sensitive governance technology described in DVE-series and STAR-POL-series documents.

### 1.2 Authority

1.2.1 Starisian holds and protects patents on DVE and SPARXSTAR technical innovations. Patent strategy and intellectual property protection decisions are within Starisian's exclusive authority.

---

## 2. Patent Family Overview

### 2.1 Patent Family C — Multi-Tiered Executable Governance

2.1.1 Patent Family C covers the technical architecture of the DVE governance enforcement mechanisms, including:

(a) Personal Policy Token — the short-lived contributor governance preference instrument;
(b) ArtifactGovernanceDeclaration — the persistent, tamper-evident governance record;
(c) Three-tier token resolution — the hierarchical resolution of Personal Policy, Group Policy, and institutional governance authority;
(d) Cryptographic governance declarations that travel with governed artifacts.

2.1.2 Documents in this repository reference Patent Family C concepts by name only. Technical implementation details, claim language, and engineering specifications relating to Patent Family C are not contained in governance documents.

2.1.3 Personnel drafting or reviewing governance documents must not add technical implementation details, code samples, or claim language to any governance document. If uncertain whether information is patent-sensitive, the matter must be escalated to Starisian's legal counsel before inclusion.

### 2.2 Governance Interoperability

2.2.1 The DVE governance model is a Starisian proprietary capability subject to Starisian's patent rights. Deployment of the DVE governance model for non-AIWA institutions requires a Starisian authorisation as defined in DVE-FRM-01, Section 12.2.

## 3. Contributor Works and Cultural Materials

### 3.1 Non-Ownership of Contributor Works

3.1.1 Starisian Technologies does not own and does not claim ownership of any contributor work, folklore, traditional cultural expression, or cultural material processed within the DVE governance pipeline.

3.1.2 Starisian's technical custody of governed artifacts within the DVE is infrastructure custody — it does not confer intellectual property rights over the content of governed artifacts.

3.1.3 AI processing of a governed Work within DVE does not transfer ownership of that Work, or of any rights in that Work, to Starisian Technologies. Computational processing is an infrastructure operation. It does not create a new chain of title or grant Starisian any ownership interest in the processed Work or in any output derived from it.

### 3.2 Governance Document Ownership

3.2.1 AIWA governance documents issued under AIWA authority are the intellectual property of AI West Africa. Documents issued under joint AIWA–Starisian authority are the intellectual property of both institutions jointly, as specified in each document's closing copyright notice. Starisian's participation in drafting joint governance documents does not grant Starisian governance authority over AIWA's governance decisions.
